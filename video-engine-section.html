02 · VIDEO ENGINE

How every Shop video gets made

This is the engine from the UGC Agent Farm, the one that made Glam's best UGC videos. Every Shop run goes through these 5 steps in order. No step gets skipped, and the person who makes a video never checks it.

  1. 1 · Pinterest Scout
    Finds the outfit or product look for the run's mode. Copies its picture. No price checks here.
  2. 2 · Image Agent: lock her face
    Slot 1 = one headshot (9:16). Slot 2 = ONE 9:16 body sheet with front, three-quarter, left and right views of the same person in the same outfit on one photo. She is not locked until both slots are filled. Make the outfit photo and the outfit sheet in chat first and check face match and outfit match.
  3. 3 · Story Agent
    Writes the hook (first 3 seconds, CTA up front), the shots and the script. Scenes are action only. Every scene is labeled with the run's one product presentation choice.
  4. 4 · Video Agent
    Consistent Character with BOTH references every scene (Reference Two is never empty). Scenes in script order, 9:16. Trim dead air at the start and end of every clip. Returns the video for checking. Never posts.
  5. 5 · Caption Agent
    TikTok caption, on-screen hook and hashtags. No price, discount or sale claim unless a live price was verified. Disclose post content ON and AI-generated label ON at posting. Commercially cleared music for Shop videos.
The only scene prompt shape allowed

"Use the reference images and keep the character perfectly consistent. [Scene action only.] [Spoken line, if any.] [Presentation sentence.]"

Never describe her face, hair, skin, body or outfit in a scene prompt. Describing her overrides the references and makes a different woman.

One product presentation per run (pick one)
  • Wearing: "Wearing the product, not holding it."
  • Holding: "Holding the product, not wearing it."
  • Using: "Using the product on camera, not just holding it."
  • None: no sentence.

Never wearing and holding the same item in one shot. A mixed run uses separate scenes: holding first, wearing later.

Video modes (a setting, not a separate farm)

Get Ready With Me · Spend The Day With Me · Tutorials · Makeup tutorial · Hair tutorial · Product review · Cooking

Director QC (separate checker) before anything reaches Glam
  • Same woman in every clip: face, hair, body, jewelry
  • One presentation way, label readable (not gibberish)
  • Hook in the first 3 seconds, no dead air, the story makes sense
  • No unverified prices, no crowns, nothing extra

Pass goes to Glam's approval page. Fail goes back to the maker with timestamps.
